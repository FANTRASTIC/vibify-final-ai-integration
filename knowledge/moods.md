happy: Happy songs are upbeat and bright. They usually pair medium-to-high energy with high valence (0.75 or more) and a danceable pulse.

chill: Chill songs are relaxed and unhurried. Expect low-to-medium energy (about 0.3 to 0.5), slow tempos and moderate valence; they work as background music.

intense: Intense songs are driving and forceful. Energy is high (0.85 or more), valence is often middling or low, and tempo is fast.

moody: Moody songs are atmospheric and slightly dark. Energy can be medium to high, but valence is low to middling (around 0.35 to 0.5), giving a brooding feel.

focused: Focused songs keep attention steady without demanding it. Energy is low to medium, rhythms are repetitive, and there are few sudden changes.

relaxed: Relaxed songs are calm and easygoing. Energy is low (about 0.35 to 0.45), tempos are slow, and valence is warm.

confident: Confident songs are assertive and strong. Energy is medium to high, rhythms are firm, and valence is neutral to positive.

peaceful: Peaceful songs are very calm and gentle. Energy is very low (below 0.3), acousticness is high, and there is little rhythmic push.

angry: Angry songs are aggressive and harsh. Energy is extremely high, valence is very low (below 0.3), and tempos are fast.

nostalgic: Nostalgic songs are wistful and reflective. Energy is low to medium, valence is middling, and the sound is often warm and acoustic.

romantic: Romantic songs are warm and intimate. Energy is medium, valence is moderately high, and the groove is smooth.

euphoric: Euphoric songs are ecstatic and peak-time. Energy is very high, valence is high, and they build to release.

laid-back: Laid-back songs are easygoing and sunny. Energy is low to medium, tempos are slow, and valence is high.

melancholic: Melancholic songs are sad but contemplative. Energy is low, valence is low (about 0.3 to 0.4), and acousticness is often high.

sad: Sad songs are sorrowful and heavy. Energy is usually low, valence is very low (below 0.3), and tempos are slow.
