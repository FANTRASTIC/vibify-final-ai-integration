Rock is guitar, bass and drums driven. Energy is high, usually 0.7 to 0.95, tempos run from 120 to 160 BPM, and acousticness is low because the guitars are amplified. Common moods are intense, confident and nostalgic, and valence is often middling or dark.

Listeners expect a strong backbeat, riffs and a loud, live-band feel. Ballads exist but are rarer.

Rock is frequently confused with metal, which is heavier, faster and darker, and with blues, which is its slower root. Punk and grunge are subgenres that keep rock's instruments but change its attitude and polish.
