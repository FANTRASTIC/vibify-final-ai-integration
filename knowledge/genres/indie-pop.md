Indie pop pairs pop's melodic hooks with a looser, more homemade sound and often guitars or soft synths. Energy is medium, around 0.5 to 0.75, tempo is 100 to 125 BPM, and acousticness is moderate. Moods are usually happy or romantic, with warm and sometimes quirky lyrics.

Listeners expect upbeat but personal-sounding songs, less glossy than the charts.

It is easily confused with mainstream pop, which is more heavily produced, and with folk or lofi, which share its gentle, intimate tone. Dream pop and bedroom pop lean softer and more atmospheric.
