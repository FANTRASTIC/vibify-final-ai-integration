Lofi is quiet, loop-based music made to sit in the background. Energy is low, around 0.3 to 0.45, tempo is slow at roughly 70 to 90 BPM, and moods are chill or focused. Acousticness is fairly high because of mellow keys, vinyl crackle and soft samples.

Listeners use it for studying, reading and late-night work, so they expect nothing sudden: no big drops, no shouting vocals, steady and repetitive grooves.

It is often confused with ambient, which has even less rhythm, and with jazz or hip hop, which supply the chords and drum patterns that lofi borrows. Trip hop and chillhop are close relatives of lofi.
