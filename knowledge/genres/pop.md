Pop is built for broad appeal: short songs, strong hooks and polished production. Typical energy is medium to high, tempo sits around 100 to 130 BPM, and the mood is usually happy or upbeat, with low acousticness because of synthesizers and studio processing.

Listeners expect catchy choruses and a danceable pulse. Slower, melancholic pop exists, but it is the exception rather than the rule.

Pop is often confused with indie pop, which is softer and more guitar-driven, and with dance music such as EDM, which pushes the same hooks at higher intensity. Regional styles such as K-pop and J-pop share pop's polish and hook-first writing.
