Jazz is built on improvisation, swung rhythm and rich chords played on acoustic instruments such as piano, upright bass, saxophone and trumpet. Energy ranges widely from relaxed to lively, tempo is often 90 to 130 BPM, and acousticness is high. Moods include relaxed, romantic and confident.

Listeners expect skilled musicianship and warm, organic sound, often in a cafe or late-night setting.

Jazz is commonly confused with blues, from which it grew, with soul and r&b, which borrow its harmony, and with lofi, which samples jazz chords over simple drum loops.
