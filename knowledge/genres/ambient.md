Ambient music emphasizes texture and atmosphere over melody and beat. Energy is very low, often below 0.3, tempo is slow or free-floating around 55 to 65 BPM, and danceability is minimal. Acousticness is high in feel, thanks to soft pads and natural sounds, and the mood is peaceful, chill or occasionally moody.

Listeners expect long evolving sounds that fade into the background for sleep, meditation or concentration.

It is often confused with lofi, which adds a steady drum loop, and with modern classical music, which shares its calm but relies on acoustic instruments and written melodies.
