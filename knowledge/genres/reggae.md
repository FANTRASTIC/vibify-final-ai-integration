Reggae is a Jamaican genre defined by offbeat guitar chops, a prominent bassline and a relaxed, rolling tempo. Energy is low to medium, tempo is slow at around 70 to 95 BPM, and acousticness is moderate. Moods are laid-back, relaxed or happy, with warm valence and themes of community and resistance.

Listeners expect an easygoing groove that is still danceable.

It is often confused with ska, which is faster, with dancehall and reggaeton, which are more electronic and rhythmically dense, and with generic summer pop.
