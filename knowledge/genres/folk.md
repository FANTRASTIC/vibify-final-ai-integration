Folk is acoustic, story-centered music built on guitar, banjo, fiddle or voice. Energy is usually low to medium, around 0.25 to 0.5, but dance-oriented folk can run lively. Tempo varies from slow waltzes to fast jigs, acousticness is very high at 0.8 or more, and moods are peaceful, melancholic or nostalgic.

Listeners expect honest lyrics, simple arrangements and an organic, handmade sound.

It is often confused with country, which shares its instruments, and with indie pop and singer-songwriter music, which are more modern in production. Celtic and traditional styles are folk's older regional forms.
