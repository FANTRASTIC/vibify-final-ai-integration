Metal is a heavier, louder offshoot of rock with distorted guitars, fast double-bass drumming and often growled or screamed vocals. Energy is extremely high, above 0.85, tempo is typically 140 to 180 BPM, and acousticness is nearly zero. Moods are angry or intense, and valence is low.

Listeners expect aggression, technical playing and sheer volume; gentle moments are brief.

Metal is commonly confused with hard rock, which is less extreme, and with punk, which is fast but simpler. Some listeners also group it with high-intensity electronic music because of its sheer force.
