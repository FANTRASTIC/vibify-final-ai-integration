Country is storytelling music built on acoustic and steel guitars, fiddle and clear vocals. Energy is low to medium, tempo is often 85 to 115 BPM, and acousticness is moderately high. Moods range from nostalgic and sad to cheerful, with themes of home, work, love and loss.

Listeners expect narrative lyrics, a steady two-beat or waltz rhythm and a warm, down-to-earth sound.

It is often confused with folk, with which it shares instruments and storytelling, and with blues, from which it borrows its bent notes and heartache. Modern country rock leans toward rock and pop production.
