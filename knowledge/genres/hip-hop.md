Hip hop centers on rhythmic spoken or rapped vocals over sampled or programmed beats and heavy bass. Energy is medium to high, tempo is slow-to-mid at about 80 to 100 BPM (trap often feels double-time), danceability is high, and acousticness is very low. Moods are confident, happy or sometimes melancholic.

Listeners expect strong grooves, lyrical delivery and bass-forward production.

It is often confused with r&b, which is sung rather than rapped, and with lofi, which reuses hip hop drum patterns without vocals. Pop frequently borrows its beats.
