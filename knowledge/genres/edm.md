EDM, or electronic dance music, is produced with synthesizers and drum machines for clubs and festivals. Energy is very high, above 0.85, tempo is usually 120 to 150 BPM with a four-on-the-floor kick, danceability is very high, and acousticness is near zero. Moods are euphoric or intense, with build-ups and drops.

Listeners expect relentless rhythm, big bass and peak-time excitement.

It is often confused with pop, which uses similar synths at lower intensity, and with synthwave, which shares its sound palette but is slower and more nostalgic. House, techno and trance are subgenres with different tempos.
