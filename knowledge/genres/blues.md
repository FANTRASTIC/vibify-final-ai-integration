Blues is built on the twelve-bar form, bent guitar notes and emotive vocals about hardship and longing. Energy is typically low to medium, tempo is slow at around 65 to 100 BPM, valence is low, and acousticness is moderate to high, from acoustic delta styles to electric Chicago blues. Moods are sad, nostalgic or gritty.

Listeners expect raw emotion and expressive instrumental solos.

It is often confused with jazz, which grew alongside it, with rock, which electrified it and sped it up, and with country and soul, which share its themes. Blues rock turns the intensity up considerably.
