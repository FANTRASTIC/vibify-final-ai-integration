R&B combines smooth sung vocals with groove-heavy rhythm sections, warm bass and layered harmonies. Energy is medium, around 0.45 to 0.6, tempo is slow to mid at about 80 to 100 BPM, danceability is high, and acousticness is low to moderate. Moods are romantic, moody or sensual.

Listeners expect emotional vocals, a relaxed groove and intimate lyrics about relationships.

It is often confused with hip hop, with which it shares beats and often collaborators, with soul and jazz, which supply its vocal style and chords, and with pop, which borrows its production.
