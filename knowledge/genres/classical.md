Classical music is composed for acoustic instruments or voice and performed from written scores. Energy is usually low to medium, with quiet piano or strings around 0.15 to 0.35 and fast orchestral movements higher. Acousticness is the highest of any genre at roughly 0.9 or more, and danceability is low. Moods are peaceful, melancholic or occasionally joyful.

Tempo varies enormously, from slow adagios near 60 BPM to quick allegros above 130.

Listeners expect dynamic contrast and no vocals or drums. It is often confused with ambient and with film or neoclassical music, which borrow its instruments and calm.
