genre: synthwave

---
Synthwave recreates the sound of 1980s film scores and arcade games with analog-style synthesizers, gated drums and pulsing basslines. Energy is medium to high, tempo is typically 100 to 125 BPM, acousticness is very low, and the mood is moody, nostalgic or driving.

Listeners expect neon, nighttime imagery and a steady, cinematic groove that suits driving or gaming.

It is often confused with EDM, which is faster and built around drops, and with 1980s pop, which supplied its melodies. Vaporwave and retrowave are close variants with slightly different tempos and moods.
