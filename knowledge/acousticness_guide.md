Acousticness guide. Acousticness is a 0 to 1 value estimating how much of a track uses acoustic instruments rather than electronic or amplified ones. Boundaries below are the 33rd and 67th percentiles of the 49-song catalog in data/songs.csv, computed from the file (statistics.quantiles, n=3, inclusive method).

Low acousticness means 0.25 or below: electronic and amplified music such as edm, metal, pop, hip hop, rock and synthwave. In scoring, a listener who does not like acoustic music has a target of 0.2.

Mid acousticness means above 0.25 and up to 0.76: blended sounds such as indie pop, reggae, blues and some r&b and country.

High acousticness means above 0.76: piano, guitar and string music such as classical, folk, ambient, jazz and lofi. In scoring, a listener who likes acoustic music has a target of 0.8.

The scorer uses only two targets (0.2 and 0.8) because the profile stores a yes or no preference rather than an exact amount.
