Energy guide. Energy is a 0 to 1 value describing how intense and active a track feels. Boundaries below are the 33rd and 67th percentiles of the 49-song catalog in data/songs.csv, computed from the file (statistics.quantiles, n=3, inclusive method).

Low energy means 0.42 or below: quiet, slow music such as ambient, classical, lofi and folk. Listeners asking for studying, sleeping, relaxing or calm sounds want low energy.

Mid energy means above 0.42 and up to 0.76: moderate music such as jazz, country, r&b, reggae and indie pop. Mixed requests like "nothing sleepy but not a workout" fall here.

High energy means above 0.76: loud, fast, driving music such as rock, metal, edm, synthwave and upbeat pop. Gym, running and party requests want high energy, roughly 0.8 to 0.95.

The catalog energy range is 0.15 to 0.97, so very low targets below 0.15 cannot be matched closely.
